🌌 Why Space Is Silent

Published: SicFacts Space Desk | Astronomy

Many movies show huge explosions in space with loud sounds, but in reality space is completely silent.

Sound travels through vibrations in air, water, or other materials. Since space is mostly a vacuum, there is no medium for sound waves to travel through.

Even if a massive star exploded nearby, astronauts would not hear the blast directly.

Astronauts communicate using radios because radio waves can travel through the vacuum of space.

Scientists use special instruments to convert signals from space into sounds that humans can hear and study.

The silence of space is one of the many fascinating differences between Earth and the universe beyond our planet.
