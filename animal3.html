🐬 Dolphins Have Unique Names

Published: SicFacts Animal Desk | Marine Science

Scientists have discovered that dolphins use unique signature whistles that function much like human names.

Each dolphin develops its own special whistle during its early life and uses it to identify itself to others.

Researchers found that dolphins can recognize and respond to the signature whistles of friends and family members even after years of separation.

When dolphins hear a familiar whistle, they often respond by repeating it, similar to calling someone by their name.

This remarkable communication system demonstrates advanced intelligence and social awareness among dolphins.

The discovery provides further evidence that dolphin societies are among the most complex in the animal kingdom.
