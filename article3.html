New Deep-Sea Species Continue To Be Found

Published: SicFacts Science Desk | Ocean Discovery

The deep ocean remains one of the least explored regions on Earth. Scientists estimate that millions of marine species have yet to be discovered.

Using advanced submarines and underwater robots, researchers are exploring regions that were once completely inaccessible.

Recent missions have revealed unusual fish, glowing jellyfish, giant worms, and microscopic organisms capable of surviving in extreme conditions.

Many of these species live under immense pressure, freezing temperatures, and complete darkness. Some create their own light through bioluminescence.

Scientists believe these discoveries could help us understand biodiversity, evolution, and even how life might survive on distant ocean worlds.
