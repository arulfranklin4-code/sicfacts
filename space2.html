🌊 Giant Clouds of Water Exist in Space

Published: SicFacts Space Desk | Astronomy

Astronomers have discovered enormous clouds of water vapor floating in deep space.

One of the largest known water reservoirs surrounds a distant black hole located billions of light-years away from Earth.

Scientists estimate that this cloud contains trillions of times more water than all of Earth's oceans combined.

The water exists as vapor and stretches across vast regions of space.

These discoveries show that water, one of the essential ingredients for life, is more common throughout the universe than once believed.

Although this water is far beyond our reach, it helps scientists understand how galaxies, stars, and planets form over time.
