🐙 Octopuses Dream While Sleeping

Published: SicFacts Animal Desk | Marine Biology

Scientists have observed fascinating behavior in sleeping octopuses that suggests they may experience dream-like states.

While resting, octopuses rapidly change color, texture, and body patterns, even though they remain asleep.

Researchers believe these changes may reflect memories or experiences being processed by the octopus brain.

Some sleeping octopuses have displayed hunting colors and movements, leading scientists to wonder if they are reliving past events.

Octopuses are among the most intelligent invertebrates on Earth, capable of solving puzzles and escaping complex enclosures.

Although scientists cannot know exactly what an octopus experiences while sleeping, the evidence suggests their minds may be far more complex than once believed.
